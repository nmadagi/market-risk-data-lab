# data

Six synthetic risk factor time series, one row per business day from
2020-01-02 to 2026-08-31 (1,738 rows), generated by `generate.py` from a
fixed seed. A high volatility era is engineered into 2022 so the stressed
VaR search has something real to find. Nothing here is vendor data.

Run `python -m data.export` to regenerate the CSVs. A test asserts the
CSV matches the generator, so the snapshot cannot drift from the app.

## market_data_clean.csv

The golden copy: what the bank's official history should look like.

| column | what it is | unit | typical level |
|---|---|---|---|
| date | business day | YYYY-MM-DD | |
| usd2y | 2 year USD swap rate | percent | 1 to 4 |
| usd5y | 5 year USD swap rate | percent | 1 to 7 |
| usd10y | 10 year USD swap rate | percent | 2 to 6 |
| swaption_vol | implied volatility of an option on the swap rate | vol points | 70 to 165 |
| eurusd | euro to dollar exchange rate | dollars per euro | 0.84 to 1.27 |
| credit_spread | extra yield a company pays over the government | basis points | 48 to 158 |

Rates and the spread are simulated as mean reverting processes in level
space. Implied vol mean reverts in log space. EURUSD is a driftless
random walk in log space. All four get extra volatility inside the 2022
stress era.

## market_data_corrupted.csv

The same history with four realistic data faults injected. This is what
the pipeline receives.

| fault | series | where | what it looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| stale | usd5y | 2026-06-01, 15 days | the value freezes at the last good print |
| spike | usd5y | 2026-07-15 | one print 8 sigma away, back to normal next day |
| gap | credit_spread | 2026-05-04, 20 days | missing values |
| splice | swaption_vol | all history before 2023-01-16 | pre-seam level shifted up 12 vol points |

## fault_log.csv

One row per injected fault: type, series, start, end, description.

## portfolio.csv

The toy trading book, held as sensitivities. Rates and spread in dollars
per basis point, vega in dollars per vol point, FX as a dollar notional.
Long the 5 year against the 2 year, long vega, long euro, long credit.
